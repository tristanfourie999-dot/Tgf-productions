How a project runs

From raw footage to final master.

01

Ingest & Sync

Footage, audio and camera reports come in, get organised and synced so the whole timeline is clean before a single cut is made.

02

Assembly

A first cut built for structure and story — we agree on the shape of the piece before polishing anything.

03

Fine Cut & Grade

Pacing tightens, colour is graded shot to shot, and titles or motion elements are built into the timeline.

04

Mix & Master

Sound is mixed, the final master is exported in every format you need, and the project is archived.
